- IPA and deb archives may expand to 8 GiB, in one entry or in all, across up to 400,000 entries. The limit was 1 GiB and 50,000 entries, which refused large games such as the one in vphone-cli#637. A refused archive now reports how far it expands, for example `archive expands to 8.79 GiB, over the 8 GiB limit`.
