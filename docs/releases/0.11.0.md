- `device passcode status | unlock | forget` (from [PR #16](https://github.com/owngoal-dev/icli/pull/16)) unlocks the keybag through `MKBUnlockDevice` with a passcode the caller supplies, for test automation. The passcode is read from stdin (`--passcode-stdin`), a 0600 file (`--passcode-file`) or a no-echo terminal prompt, never from argv. icli never generates or iterates passcodes.
- To prevent lockouts, a ledger at `/var/mobile/Library/Caches/com.icli.icli/passcode-ledger.json` stores salted PBKDF2 digests of rejected passcodes. A passcode already in the ledger is rejected without reaching the keybag (exit status 4). icli also refuses (exit status 5) once the larger of the ledger's count and the keybag's failed-attempt counter reaches `--max-failures` (default 3, at most 4, below iOS's first delay), or while the keybag backs off. A success empties the ledger. Run `forget --force` after the device passcode changes. Every answer includes `attempts_remaining` and a `hint`. Details are in [the design notes](https://github.com/owngoal-dev/icli/blob/0.11.0/docs/development/passcode-unlock.md).
- Unlocking the keybag does not dismiss SpringBoard's lock screen; `lock_screen` in the answer reports whether it is still up.
- `icli probe` adds the read-only `api('passcode.status')`.
- The CLI signing profile adds `com.apple.keystore.device`, which SpringBoard, demod and sharingd hold on iOS 27.0.1. Hosts that call `unlockDevice` through IcliKit need it too.
- Checked on a physical iPhone 15 Pro running iOS 17.3.1 with RootHide: a wrong passcode counted once and was then fast-rejected without another attempt; the right passcode unlocked the keybag and reset the counter. The new host test `scripts/test-passcode-ledger.sh` runs in CI. This release did not rerun the full device regression.
