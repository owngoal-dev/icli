This repository is the packaging around libarchive. The packaging is MIT. The
binaries it publishes are built from four upstream projects, all of which
require their notice to be reproduced in a binary redistribution; those notices
follow in full.

Everything vendored here is permissively licensed. liblzo2 is deliberately
excluded because it is GPLv2. Only the libraries of zstd and lz4 are built --
their command line tools, which are GPLv2, are not.

zlib, bzip2, iconv and libxml2 are linked from the Apple SDKs rather than
vendored, so no copy of them is redistributed here.
